## Generated music — local preview

Open Skies ambience was generated for HackUniverse with ElevenLabs `music_v2_5`. Its prompt requests a sparse, optimistic major-key soundscape without vocals or choir, without imitating a named artist, franchise or soundtrack. Its source, processed FLAC master and runtime MP3 are unchanged by the sound-effect replacement. The FLAC is an edit of a generated MP3, not an original lossless generation source. [generation.json](../assets/audio/generation.json) retains the music request, and [the processed manifest](../assets/audio/manifest.json) records measurements and hashes. Generation provenance does not establish plan-specific publication approval; consult the applicable [Eleven Music terms](https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms) and [ElevenCreative terms](https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms).

## Imported Starmap effects — local preview

The supplied `ARK_Starmap/Assets/Sounds` collection contains 45 WAV recordings. Its README describes an offline Unity remake of the ARK Starmap website. No LICENSE file was found in the supplied project, so this import does not assert a redistribution license or verified individual authorship. No generation request was made for these recordings, and no generation prompts are invented.

Canonical copies preserve the original folder hierarchy under [imported/starmap](../assets/audio/imported/starmap), with identical decoded PCM samples, sample rate, channels and frame count. Descriptive/BWF tags are removed from these copies; the supplied source files remain untouched. [The import catalog](../assets/audio/imported/starmap/catalog.json) records each original relative filename and file hash, cleaned-file hash, PCM identity, removed tag names and derivative edits. Removing technical metadata does not change authorship or licensing.

MP3 derivatives live in [the web catalog](../web/public/audio/catalog/starmap), with nine active aliases under [audio/sfx](../web/public/audio/sfx). Menu actions use `UI_DiscOpen`, `UI_DiscClose` and `UI_DiscClick`; camera movement uses `CAM_Rotate` and `CAM_Zoom`; toggle states use `ToggleButtonActivate`/`ToggleButtonDeactivate`, and destination hover uses `GOHover`. Both zoom directions share the same derivative bytes. Constant gain, short endpoint fades and limited quiet-padding trims adapt these supplied recordings for playback, without changing speed or pitch. ID3, Xing and encoder tags are omitted; measured MP3 durations include ordinary codec frame padding.

`AmbientMusic.ogg` and Unity `.meta` files are excluded entirely. Open Skies remains the soundtrack. `SystemAmbient` and the `Unused` recordings are catalog-only, without new feature bindings or full-catalog preloading. Eight supplied WAVs contain digital silence and remain silent rather than being filled with invented audio.

The previous generated and user-supplied effects, including the cinematic zoom and cyberpunk camera clip, are preserved with their source/runtime copies and metadata in [drafts/pre-starmap](../assets/audio/drafts/pre-starmap). Earlier candidates remain in [assets/audio/drafts](../assets/audio/drafts); none is selected by the active aliases. [The audio guide](UNIVERSE-AUDIO.md) separates provenance and technical checks from listening, browser and physical-device validation. Audio does not inherit the spacecraft's CC BY license or an inferred repository-wide license.
