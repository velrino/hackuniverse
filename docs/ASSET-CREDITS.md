## Generated and supplied audio — local preview

Open Skies ambience was generated for HackUniverse with ElevenLabs `music_v2_5`; menu-open, menu-close and selection use `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`. Their original descriptive prompts request a sparse, optimistic major-key soundscape without vocals/choir and restrained aerospace microclicks/electrical ticks, without imitating a named artist, franchise or soundtrack. These four recordings remain unchanged by the zoom replacement.

The two zoom cues share one user-provided recording: `Futuristic_cinematic_#3-1793355528036.mp3`, preserved byte-for-byte as [user-zoom-cinematic.mp3](../assets/audio/source/user-zoom-cinematic.mp3). Its exact supplied prompt is retained in the `imports` record of [generation.json](../assets/audio/generation.json); no new generation request was made for it. Both runtime directions keep the complete approximately 0.6065-second clip, with no reversal or pitch change, attenuated 5 dB and given short endpoint fades. This records supplied provenance without asserting a provider-generated origin or publication license. Canonical recordings live under [assets/audio/source](../assets/audio/source).

The current status is local preview awaiting user listening validation. The [processed manifest](../assets/audio/manifest.json) is the record for the current music edit, runtime derivatives, measurements and hashes; [the audio guide](UNIVERSE-AUDIO.md) separates these technical records from listening and browser validation. The FLAC master is a processed edit of the generated MP3, not a lossless generation source. Previous candidates, including superseded technical zoom cues, are preserved in [assets/audio/drafts](../assets/audio/drafts) and are not runtime assets. No plan-specific approval for generated audio or publication rights for supplied audio are recorded here. The applicable [Eleven Music terms](https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms), [ElevenCreative terms](https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms) and supplied recording's rights record must be checked against intended distribution. Audio is not assigned the spacecraft's CC BY license or an inferred repository-wide license.
